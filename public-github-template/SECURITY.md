# OTYA Security Policy

Security reports should be handled privately. Otya is the public umbrella brand; Otya Holdings Limited is the intended parent entity and its URSB registration is in progress.

## Report a vulnerability

Email **security@otya.site** with a clear subject such as `OTYA security report`.

Please include:

- affected OTYA product or public endpoint
- a concise description of the issue
- safe reproduction steps
- expected and observed behavior
- impact you believe is possible
- any non-sensitive supporting evidence

## Do not

- publish credentials, tokens, private keys, OTPs or personal data in a public issue
- access or modify data that is not yours
- disrupt production services or intentionally exhaust resources
- use social engineering against users or staff
- publicly disclose an unresolved vulnerability before a reasonable remediation period

## Scope

The official Otya website, Otya Media and connected Otya services may be in scope when operated or controlled by Otya. Third-party services are governed by their own security programs.

Otya will acknowledge good-faith reports and prioritize issues based on demonstrated risk.
